## How It Works

1. Send TRX to the TronZap platform address.
2. Energy is delegated to your wallet automatically within one minute, available for 1 hour.
3. The amount of energy depends on the amount of TRX sent.

Alternatively, register an account at [TronZap Dashboard](https://dash.tronzap.com/) to top up your balance and buy energy instantly without QR codes or delays.

## Pricing

- **65,000 Energy** (standard USDT transfer): ~3 TRX (~$0.69)
- **131,000 Energy** (transfer to new/inactive wallet): ~5.50 TRX (~$1.27)
- Without energy, a USDT transfer costs ~13.40 TRX. With TronZap energy, ~3 TRX. **Savings: ~10.40 TRX per transfer.**

We recommend always buying 131,000 energy to ensure your USDT payment goes through without extra blockchain fees.

## FAQ

- **What is TronZap?** Automated TRON energy leasing platform operating 24/7. Non-custodial, safe, no intermediaries. Member of TRON DAO.
- **What is OUT_OF_ENERGY?** Error when your wallet lacks energy for smart contract execution during USDT (TRC20) transfers. Solution: rent at least 131,000 energy on TronZap before transferring.
- **How to get energy in TronLink?** Register on TronZap, go to Buy Energy, paste your TronLink address, choose amount, pay. Energy arrives instantly.
- **Can I use TronZap without registration?** Yes. Send TRX to the platform address and energy is auto-delegated within one minute.
- **How to send USDT without TRX?** Rent TRON Energy via TronZap to your wallet and transfer USDT without spending TRX.
- **How is the USDT transfer fee calculated?** TRON uses energy first; if none available, TRX is burned. Standard transfer needs 65,000 energy + 345 bandwidth. Transfers to new wallets need 131,000 energy.
- **Are purchases refundable?** No. Paid TRX goes into staking to power energy for all users.
- **Is there a dashboard?** Yes. [TronZap Dashboard](https://dash.tronzap.com/) for tracking purchases, transaction history, and managing energy.
- **Energy subscriptions?** Available via the [Telegram Bot](https://telegram.me/tronzap_bot) and [Dashboard](https://dash.tronzap.com/). Ideal for services, bots, and frequent senders.
- **TRON Bandwidth rental?** Available via [Telegram Bot](https://telegram.me/tronzap_bot) and [Dashboard](https://dash.tronzap.com/).
- **AML Checks?** Available via [Telegram Bot](https://telegram.me/tronzap_bot) and [Dashboard](https://dash.tronzap.com/) for address and transaction screening.
